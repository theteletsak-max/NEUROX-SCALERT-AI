HITMAN AI — TRADE ENTRY DISCIPLINE ENGINE v1.0
Irregular Trade Prevention System
=================================

Mission
- Never open random / emotional / duplicate / low-quality trades
- Open only when the complete trade thesis is valid

Rules
#1  Zero irregular entries — multi-confirmation (not one candle/indicator/BOS)
#2  Trade thesis validation — trend/structure/BOS/CHoCH/liq/fib/mom/prec/prob/conf
#3  Signal stability — reject flicker and one-tick signals
#4  Confirmation lock — ordered Structure→Trend→Liq→Mom→Conf→Prec→Prob
#5  Master trend lock — BUY master ⇒ BUY only; SELL master ⇒ SELL only
#6  Timeframe agreement — H4→H1→M30→M15→M5
#7  Duplicate protection — same signal/candle/tick/direction
#8  Entry quality check — location, R:R, liquidity, structure, trend
#9  Broker validation — market/connection/margin/price/volume
#10 Supreme AI approval — all required rules must pass
#11 Trade cool-down — unlock on trade done (optional new structure)
#12 Explainable thesis — "why open" must be complete

Module: TradeEntryDiscipline.mqh
Hook: UltraAIDecide (before FIRE) + OnFill after ExecuteBuy/Sell
Defaults: Enabled=true, Strict=false (soft InstantQuality-compatible)
