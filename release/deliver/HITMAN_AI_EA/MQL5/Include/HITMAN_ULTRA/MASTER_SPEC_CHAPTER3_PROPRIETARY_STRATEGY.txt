CHAPTER 3 — PROPRIETARY STRATEGY ENGINE
BUILD_ID: HA_ULTRA_93
============================================================

MISSION
  Transform raw market information into one high-quality trading thesis.
  The Strategy Engine NEVER places orders.
  The Strategy Engine NEVER closes trades.
  The Strategy Engine NEVER manages positions.
  It ONLY determines whether a valid opportunity exists
  according to the proprietary strategy family.

============================================================
1. STRATEGY CORE
  Inputs: Trend · Momentum · Volatility · Liquidity · Spread
          · Session · Event (from Market Intelligence / snap)
  Output: Trade Thesis
  API: UltraPropStrategy_Evaluate

2. MARKET CONTEXT
  BULLISH | BEARISH | NEUTRAL | TRANSITION
  API: UltraPropStrategy_MarketContext (reuses Chapter 2 picture)

3. TREND VALIDATION
  Direction · Strength · Stability · Continuation · Exhaustion
  Rule: weak trends never become high-confidence trades

4. MOMENTUM VALIDATION
  Direction · Strength · Acceleration · Continuation
  Energy required to justify participation

5. LIQUIDITY VALIDATION
  Participation · efficiency · conditions
  Supports decision quality (fake/thin soft-hard)

6. TRADE THESIS ENGINE
  Every trade answers: Why should this trade exist?
  Contains: Direction · Context · Evidence · Risk note · Confidence
  No clear thesis → No trade

7. CONFLUENCE ENGINE
  Trend + Momentum + Liquidity + Volatility + Context
  → one confluence result 0–100 (side-aligned UltraConfluence*)
  More agreement → higher confidence

8. CONFIDENCE ENGINE
  One score 0–100 from USM2 (u.score.confidence)
  Increases when independent factors agree

9. ENTRY VALIDATION → Strategy Approved
  Thesis · Trend · Momentum · Confluence · Risk requirements
  API: UltraPropStrategy_ValidateEntry

10. STRATEGY OUTPUT (and nothing else)
  BUY_CANDIDATE | SELL_CANDIDATE | WAIT
  Mission Control makes the final decision

============================================================
RULES
  ✔ One Trade Thesis · one Confidence · one Strategy Decision
  ✔ No duplicate / contradictory signals
  ✔ Strategy never executes orders
  ✔ Strategy only creates high-quality candidates
